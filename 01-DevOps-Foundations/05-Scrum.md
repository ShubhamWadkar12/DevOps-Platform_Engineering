# 🏃 Scrum Framework


Scrum is the most widely used Agile framework for managing software development projects.

It helps teams break large projects into smaller, manageable pieces called **Sprints**, allowing them to deliver working software frequently, gather feedback, and continuously improve.

Scrum promotes collaboration, transparency, and adaptability, making it ideal for modern software development.

# 📌 What is Scrum?

**Scrum** is an Agile framework used to manage and deliver software projects through short, fixed-length iterations called **Sprints**.

Each Sprint produces a working version of the product, allowing teams to deliver value continuously and improve based on feedback.

---

# 👥 Scrum Roles

Every Scrum team consists of three key roles.

## 1. Product Owner

The Product Owner represents the customer and decides what features should be built.

### Responsibilities

* Defines product requirements.
* Prioritizes the Product Backlog.
* Maximizes product value.
* Communicates with stakeholders.

---

## 2. Scrum Master

The Scrum Master helps the team follow Scrum principles and removes obstacles that slow down development.

### Responsibilities

* Facilitates Scrum meetings.
* Removes blockers.
* Coaches the team.
* Promotes Agile practices.

---

## 3. Development Team

The Development Team builds, tests, and delivers the product.

### Responsibilities

* Design the solution.
* Develop features.
* Test the application.
* Deliver working software.

---

# 📅 Scrum Events

## Sprint

A Sprint is a fixed period, usually **1–4 weeks**, during which the team develops a working product increment.

---

## Sprint Planning

The team decides:

* What work will be completed.
* How the work will be completed.

---

## Daily Scrum (Daily Stand-up)

A short **15-minute meeting** held every day.

Each team member answers:

* What did I complete yesterday?
* What will I work on today?
* Is there anything blocking my progress?

---

## Sprint Review

At the end of the Sprint, the team demonstrates the completed work to stakeholders and collects feedback.

---

## Sprint Retrospective

The team reflects on the Sprint and discusses:

* What went well?
* What could be improved?
* What actions should be taken in the next Sprint?

---

# 📦 Scrum Artifacts

## Product Backlog

A prioritized list of all features, improvements, and bug fixes required for the product.

---

## Sprint Backlog

A list of Product Backlog items selected for the current Sprint.

---

## Product Increment

The completed, tested, and potentially releasable work delivered at the end of a Sprint.

---

# 🔄 Scrum Workflow

```text
Product Backlog
       ↓
Sprint Planning
       ↓
Sprint Backlog
       ↓
Sprint (1–4 Weeks)
       ↓
Working Product Increment
       ↓
Sprint Review
       ↓
Sprint Retrospective
       ↓
Next Sprint
```

# 🌍 Real-World Example

Imagine a team is building an **Online Food Delivery App**.

### Sprint 1

* User Registration
* Login

### Sprint 2

* Restaurant Listing
* Search

### Sprint 3

* Shopping Cart
* Checkout

### Sprint 4

* Payment Gateway
* Order Tracking

After each Sprint, users can test the new features and provide feedback.

# ✅ Advantages of Scrum

* Faster software delivery.
* Better teamwork.
* Continuous customer feedback.
* Improved product quality.
* Easy to adapt to changes.
* Better visibility of project progress.

---

# ⚠️ Limitations of Scrum

* Requires active collaboration.
* Needs experienced and disciplined teams.
* Frequent meetings may feel excessive for very small projects.
* Scope changes can affect Sprint planning if not managed properly.

# 💼 Interview Questions

- **What is Scrum?**  
  Scrum is an Agile framework used to develop software through short, iterative sprints.

- **What is a Sprint?**  
  A Sprint is a fixed time period (usually 1–4 weeks) in which a specific set of work is completed.

- **What are the three Scrum roles?**  
  Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Development Team.

- **What is the Product Backlog?**  
  The Product Backlog is a prioritized list of all features, improvements, and bug fixes for the product.

- **What is the Sprint Backlog?**  
  The Sprint Backlog is the list of tasks selected from the Product Backlog to be completed during the current Sprint.

- **What happens during a Daily Scrum?**  
  The team holds a short daily meeting to discuss progress, plans, and any blockers.

- **What is the difference between Sprint Review and Sprint Retrospective?**  
  Sprint Review checks the completed work with stakeholders, while Sprint Retrospective focuses on improving the team's process for the next Sprint.
